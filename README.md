## Overview

ALK △ (School of Integrated Consciousness) is an independent research framework focused on the study of complex systems as dynamic processes evolving over time.

The work explores the relationship between **stability, structural drift, and memory**, and how systems develop through continuous interaction with their environment.

Rather than treating disciplines as isolated domains, this framework approaches them as interconnected expressions of a unified analytical structure.

---

## Core Research Domains

The research is structured around three interconnected domains:

### 1. Complex Systems Analysis
Study of systems as evolving processes shaped by internal dynamics and environmental interaction.

### 2. Cognitive Models in Artificial Intelligence
Development of adaptive models based on memory, interaction, and dynamic decision formation.

### 3. Theoretical Explorations in Quantum Systems
Investigation of quantum systems within open environments, focusing on stability and interaction.

---

## Research Direction

The central objective is to understand how systems behave under changing conditions, with emphasis on:

- Dynamic stability and structural drift  
- Pattern formation and transformation  
- Memory as a governing factor in system evolution  

This work does not aim to provide fixed models, but to establish a methodological approach applicable across multiple domains.

---

## Conceptual Foundation

The framework is based on the principle that:

> Stability is not a static state, but a dynamic equilibrium emerging from continuous interaction.

Systems are analyzed through three core elements:

- Pattern  
- Flow  
- Memory  

These elements define how systems evolve, adapt, and respond to change over time.
